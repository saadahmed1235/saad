## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a premium, authoritative, and futuristic digital agency presence tailored for enterprise decision-makers, high-growth tech ventures, and luxury digital initiatives. The design ethos fuses dark-mode minimalism with refined glassmorphism and subtle cyberpunk radiance. 

Visual themes balance deep spatial darkness with vibrant, focused energy vectors:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian and midnight navy canvas layers evoke quiet prestige, technical mastery, and structural infinity.
- **Accents:** High-frequency electric violet transitioning into neon cyber cyan injects forward-looking dynamism without overwhelming content clarity.
- **Glass & Depth:** Ultra-thin translucent surface tiers, crisp frosted blurs, and sub-pixel edge highlights simulate multi-layered optical glass interfaces.
- **Tone & Response:** Confident, intelligent, frictionless, and exceptionally polished.

## Colors
The palette is engineered for a dark canvas with layered luminosity.

- **Background Foundations:**
  - Base Obsidian: `#090A0F` (Root application canvas)
  - Midnight Navy: `#0B0F19` (Elevated canvas and section alternation)
  - Surface Glass: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` (Card and container base fill)
  - Surface Glass Hover: `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.75)`
- **Key Brand Accents:**
  - Primary Electric Violet: `#8B5CF6` (Dominant actions, primary interactive states, glow cores)
  - Secondary Cyber Cyan: `#06B6D4` (Data highlights, secondary actions, high-contrast focal points)
  - Tertiary Neon Magenta: `#EC4899` (Used sparingly for micro-badges, alerts, or terminal accents)
  - Linear Signature Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6 0%, #06B6D4 100%)`
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - Text Primary: `#F8FAFC` (Pure optical clarity on dark surfaces)
  - Text Secondary: `#94A3B8` (Descriptions, metadata, de-emphasized values)
  - Text Muted: `#64748B` (Placeholders, inactive indicators, borders)
- **Stroke & Optical Borders:**
  - Glass Border Default: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - Glass Border Active / Violet: `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`
  - Glass Border Cyan Highlight: `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.45)`

## Typography
Typographic scale unites **Plus Jakarta Sans** for expressive, modern, geometric headlining with **Inter** for dense, razor-sharp functional legibility.

- **Headlines:** Display and headline levels rely on tight letter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver architectural impact across dark surfaces.
- **Body & Data:** Inter provides neutral optical clarity at standard and reduced sizes, preserving contrast even on tinted translucent backgrounds.
- **Labels & Tags:** Uppercase or high-tracking small text tokens (`label-caps`) are reserved for category tagging, live status indicators, and technical metrics.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a responsive 12-column fluid grid system with structural containment at `1440px`.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-sm: 1rem`)
  - Tablet: `768px - 1024px` (8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, `margin-desktop: 4rem`, `gutter-lg: 2rem`)
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Based on an 8pt spatial increment, with half-steps (`0.25rem`) used strictly for micro-spacing within tight pill tags and badges.
- **Section Pacing:** Vertical gaps between major portfolio showcases and capability modules range between `5rem` and `8rem`, allowing the deep canvas to ground high-fidelity visuals without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is realized entirely through optical translucency, light scattering, and localized neon luminescence rather than standard opaque dropshadows.

- **Level 1 (Subtle Panes / Inline Cards):**
  - Background: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(12px)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`
  - Shadow: `0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`
- **Level 2 (Featured Cards / Floating Bars):**
  - Background: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(20px)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`
  - Shadow: `0 12px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`
- **Level 3 (Modals / Flyouts / Active Overlays):**
  - Background: `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.85)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(32px)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`
  - Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`
- **Luminescent Accent Glows (Micro-interactions):**
  - Violet Radiant Core: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`
  - Cyan Edge Highlight: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`

## Shapes
The structural geometry merges technical precision with ergonomic curvature:

- **Base Radii:** Standard component surfaces, including cards, inputs, and content panels, leverage a `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`) base up to `1rem` for major cards and `1.5rem` for large modular containers.
- **Pill Geometry:** Pill shapes (`9999px`) are strictly applied to micro-elements: tags, category chips, status pills, and primary CTAs. This creates a tactile contrast between structured rectilinear glass viewports and fluid, clickable triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Gradient CTA:** Full pill border radius (`9999px`). Background is a linear gradient from Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`) to Cyber Cyan (`#06B6D4`). Text is pure white (`#FFFFFF`) using `label-lg`. On hover, apply a continuous radial bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.45)`) and subtle scale transform (`1.02`).
- **Glass Secondary Button:** Pill border radius, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover, shift background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` and border to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.5)`.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Glassmorphic panels built on Level 1 or Level 2 elevation.
- Standard padding: `space-xl` (desktop), `space-lg` (mobile).
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Interactive state: On cursor hover, smoothly transition the border to a gradient-derived glow (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`) and render a soft radial light tracking the cursor position.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Tags:** `border-radius: 9999px`, height `28px`, padding `0 space-sm`, typography `label-md`. Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Glow Status Badge:** Includes a live pulsing LED indicator (`6px x 6px` circle with matching color ambient ring animation). Uses Cyan (`#06B6D4`) for system active status and Violet (`#8B5CF6`) for AI model processing states.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height `48px`, `border-radius: 0.5rem`.
- Background: `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.7)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Text: `#F8FAFC`, placeholder: `#64748B`.
- Focus State: Border switches directly to Cyber Cyan (`#06B6D4`), paired with an outer focus halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base size `20px x 20px`. Checkbox has `4px` corner radius; radio is circular.
- Border: `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Background: `transparent`.
- Selected State: Background fills with Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`), border aligns with `#8B5CF6`, carrying a micro violet glow.

### Interactive Metric Display
- Dedicated portfolio component featuring massive numerals (`headline-xl`), a muted upper cap label (`label-caps`), and a bottom hairline divider in Cyan glow (`height: 1px`, `background: linear-gradient(90deg, #06B6D4 0%, transparent 100%)`).
